Our Story

Fifteen years behind the counter.

Golden Time began as a single repair bench in Pettah, fixing watches other shops had given up on. Word travelled, and the bench became a boutique.

Today we sell, service and restore timepieces for collectors across Sri Lanka — but the promise from day one hasn't changed: honest advice, fair prices, and a watch that still runs true a decade from now.

Sunil Perera Founder & Master Watchmaker
